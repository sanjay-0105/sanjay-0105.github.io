Experimental Design Types in Stated Choice Studies

IST Lab Review Meeting · Department of Civil Engineering, IISc

An overview of experimental designs for stated choice surveys, comparing full factorial, fractional factorial, orthogonal, efficient and orthogonal-in-differences designs and when each is appropriate. The talk focuses on orthogonal designs and orthogonal blocking, showing how attribute-level balance and zero correlation allow independent estimation of main effects, and how large designs can be split into blocks without losing that balance. Worked examples on a multimodal survey of public transport, private vehicles, ride-hailing and auto-rickshaws illustrate each step.
