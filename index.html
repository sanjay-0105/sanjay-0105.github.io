Sanjay Adhikary

PhD Student · Department of Civil Engineering
Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru

I am a transportation researcher at the Indian Institute of Science, where I study dynamic pricing for ride-hailing services. I bring together behavioural modelling and optimisation to understand how prices shape traveller decisions, an approach that also extends to airlines, hotels and EV battery-swapping networks. My path into transportation began with a B.Tech in Civil Engineering from Jalpaiguri Government Engineering College. It continued through an M.Tech in Environmental Engineering at IIT Guwahati, where I modelled the dispersion of air pollutants, and an M.Tech in Transportation Systems Engineering at IISc, which led me to my PhD.

Research interests: Behavioural study · Optimization · Stochastic modeling
