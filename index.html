Framework for Simulation-Based Assessment of First- and Last-Mile Fleet Sizing Strategies for a Multimodal Transit Interchange

M.Tech in Civil Engineering · Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru
Supervisor: Dr. Tarun Rambha

An agent-based simulation in AnyLogic for sizing auto-rickshaw fleets at Yeshwantpur Railway Station, Bengaluru, where mode choice across walking, auto, bus, metro and private vehicles is modelled with a multinomial logit model calibrated on IRCTC reservation data and BMTC GTFS schedules. The results show that last-mile bus use is held back by service gaps at the interchange rather than by preference, and that waiting time is the strongest policy lever, which links fleet sizing and bus scheduling as decisions that should be planned together.
